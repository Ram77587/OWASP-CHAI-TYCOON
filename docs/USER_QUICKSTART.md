# User Quickstart Guide — Chai Tycoon

Welcome to **Chai Tycoon**! Step into the apron of a roadside Chai entrepreneur and build the most celebrated Chai stall in town.

---

## 1. Quick Navigation & Daily Routine

Every day in Chai Tycoon follows a 3-step loop:

### Step 1: Morning Prep & Market Procurement
1. **Check the Weather:** Monsoons double Chai and Samosa cravings! Scorching heat slows hot drink demand.
2. **Buy Ingredients:** Stock up on *Fresh Milk, Tea Leaves, Sugar, Ginger, Cardamom,* and *Kulhad Cups*. 
3. **Set Selling Prices:** Adjust price sliders. Set reasonable prices for College Students (under ₹15) and premium prices for Techies (up to ₹25-30).
4. Click **"Open Stall"** to start the morning rush!

### Step 2: The Daytime Rush
* Customers arrive at your counter with order bubbles and patience timers.
* **To Serve:** Click the **"Serve Order"** button on the matching customer ticket or press **Keyboard Hotkeys `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`**.
* Keep your eyes on the inventory counters. If you run out of milk or cups, you cannot brew that item until tomorrow!
* Satisfied customers leave 5-star ratings and cash tips.

### Step 3: Evening P&L & Stall Upgrades
* Review your daily earnings: Gross Revenue, Ingredient Costs (COGS), Stall Rent, and Net Margin.
* Visit the **Upgrade Shop** to boost efficiency:
  * **Brass Samovar Kettle:** 25% faster brewing.
  * **Earthen Kulhads:** Boosts customer happiness and allows higher pricing.
  * **Chotu (Assistant):** Automatically helps pour and serve tea when you're busy!
  * **Street Radio:** Plays retro beats that keep waiting customers patient longer.

---

## 2. Navigating the Round 1 Supply Shock

At the end of Day 1, a breaking news crisis will hit: **Milk procurement prices double!**
You must select one of three crisis strategies:
1. **Raise Prices:** Keeps your profit safe, but beware of customer drop-offs.
2. **Absorb Margin:** Take the hit yourself to keep customers smiling and loyal.
3. **Pivot Menu:** Promote milk-free Kerala Sulaimani Chai and crispy Samosas to thrive despite the milk freeze!
